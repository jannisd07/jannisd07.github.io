Goals

Your focus time, as a place.

Goals turns focused work and real-world visits into a pixel-art island that grows with you. Finish a session and something new takes root — a tree, a house, a boat, a lighthouse. Nothing is bought; everything is earned.

Focus Timer measures time

Pick an activity and a weekly target in hours. Work in Intervals, or in Flowtime, where the clock keeps counting until you stop.

Auto Check-In measures visits

Pin one real place — your gym, your library — and Goals logs the visit when you actually go. Passing by never counts. Entirely optional.

Quiet by design

No ads, no tracking, no feed. Friends see your week and your island, and nothing else.
